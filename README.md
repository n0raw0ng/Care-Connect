<h2 id="description">Description</h2>
<p>
  Care Connect is a prototype project designed to facilitate food donations for the homeless and identify secure distribution locations. The platform streamlines coordination among NGOs, businesses, and community members to ensure efficient food aid delivery.
</p>
<p>
  This site serves as a functional demonstration of features that NGOs or businesses can integrate into their own platforms to increase visitor engagement. Key features include:
</p>
<ul>
  <li><strong>See the Impact</strong>: A prototype food scanner interface with a sliding navigation system to visualize donation outcomes.</li>
  <li><strong>Hear the Change</strong>: An interactive audio module that broadcasts current news regarding homeless issues in Malaysia, featuring controls for playback, volume, and speed.</li>
</ul>
